⚡ Flash espSAK

Install the espSAK firmware on your TTGO T-Display straight from the browser — no IDE, no drivers to configure.

1. Connect your TTGO T-Display with a USB-C data cable.
2. Click Install below and pick the serial port that appears.
3. Wait for the flash to finish, then the device reboots into espSAK.
⚠️ Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use desktop Chrome or Edge. ⚠️ This page must be served over https (or localhost).

Works only in Chrome or Edge on desktop (Web Serial). Make sure the cable carries data, not just power. If the port does not appear, install the CH9102 / CP2102 USB-serial driver for your OS.

Troubleshooting

  1. No port listed → it is a charge-only cable or the USB-serial driver is missing.
  2. Flash fails to start → hold the BOOT button (Button 2 / GPIO0), tap RESET, release BOOT, then retry.
  3. Flashing does not touch your microSD card — your /config and logs stay intact.
